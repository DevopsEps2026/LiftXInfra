CAREERS AT LIFTX INFRA

Careers | We Are Hiring

Join LiftX Infra, a growing company in crane services, infrastructure and civil construction. We are looking for skilled, reliable people to join our team, with competitive pay, a safety-focused work culture and opportunities to grow.

BUILD YOUR FUTURE WITH US

Open Positions

Find the role that fits your skills and experience. Send us your name, the position you are applying for, your experience and copies of any relevant licenses or certificates.

01Crane Operator

Role: Operate mobile, tower or crawler cranes to lift and shift loads at project sites.

Requirements: Valid crane operating license or certificate, 3+ years of experience, and knowledge of load charts and signals.

Key skills: Safe operation, daily machine checks and coordination with riggers.

Apply for this role →

02JCB Operator

Role: Operate JCB/backhoe loaders for excavation, loading, trenching and site leveling.

Requirements: Valid driving license and 2+ years of experience operating JCB or similar machines.

Key skills: Machine handling, basic maintenance and following site instructions.

Apply for this role →

03Civil Engineer

Role: Plan, execute and monitor civil and infrastructure work, including quantity estimation, drawings, quality checks and progress reporting.

Requirements: B.E./B.Tech or Diploma in Civil Engineering; 1–5 years of site experience. Freshers may apply for junior roles.

Key skills: Site management, AutoCAD, estimation and billing, and team handling.

Apply for this role →

04Civil Workers

Trades: Mason, helper, bar bender and carpenter.

Role: Support construction activities such as concreting, shuttering, bar bending, masonry and finishing.

Requirements and skills: Site experience preferred; willingness to work on-site, follow safety rules, work as a team and bring relevant trade skills.

Apply for this role →

05Supervisor

Role: Manage daily site work, labour and materials, and ensure work is completed on time and to quality standards.

Requirements: 3+ years of civil or infrastructure site experience; Diploma, ITI or equivalent preferred.

Key skills: Labour management, site reporting, safety compliance and basic drawing interpretation.

Apply for this role →
